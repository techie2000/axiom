# Entities

## Goal

View and search legal entity master data in Axiom.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in to Axiom.
- Your role includes access to the **Entities** module.

## Steps

1. Click **Entities** in the top navigation.
2. Use the **Search** bar to find an entity by name.
3. Click a row to view the full entity record.
