# Accounts

## Goal

View and search account static data in Axiom.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in to Axiom.
- Your role includes access to the **Accounts** module.

## Steps

1. Click **Accounts** in the top navigation.
2. Use the **Search** bar to find an account by name or reference.
3. Click a row to view the full account record.
