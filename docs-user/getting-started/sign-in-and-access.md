# Sign In & Access

## Goal

Access the Axiom application with your credentials and understand how to register if you do not
yet have an account.

## Prerequisites

- A supported browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari — latest version recommended).
- Your registered email address and password, or an invitation from an Axiom administrator.

## Steps

### Sign in

1. Open Axiom in your browser.
2. On the login page, enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign In**.
4. If your credentials are correct, you are redirected to the Dashboard.

### First-time registration

1. On the login page, click **Register** (or use the link in your invitation email).
2. Fill in the registration form:
   - **Name** — your display name.
   - **Email** — must be a valid address you can access.
   - **Password** — choose a strong password (minimum 8 characters).
3. Click **Create Account**.
4. Your account is created in **Pending Approval** status. An Axiom administrator must approve it
   before you can log in. You will receive a notification when your account is approved.

### Reset a forgotten password

Contact your Axiom administrator to reset your password. Self-service password reset is not
available in the current release.

## Expected result

After a successful sign-in you see the Axiom dashboard with the main navigation menu.

## Common issues

| Issue | Possible cause | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Invalid credentials" error | Wrong email or password | Check for typos; passwords are case-sensitive |
| "Account pending approval" | New account not yet approved | Contact your administrator |
| Blank page after sign-in | Browser cache issue | Clear browser cache and retry |
| Cannot register | Registration disabled | Contact your administrator |
